# Design direction
Vintage 1940s Hollywood Western field guide, inspired by comedy Western posters. Granny is a capable trailblazer, not a caricature. Original illustrations; no film poster reproductions.
Palette: espresso #302C25, barn red #963D30, gold #B98B42, parchment #F5EDDD, teal #315F5B, white #FFFFFF.
Expressive display headings; readable sans-serif body. Responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast and meaningful alt text.
Use an approved OCTC white or white-and-gold logo on a dark background, unmodified. Logo asset and brand approval pending.
Keep humor in titles and callouts; instruction remains precise.
